01

Overview

Welcome to FinCalc India (fincalcindia.in). We provide free online financial calculators for Indian users including EMI, GST, SIP, Income Tax, FD, HRA, Gratuity, and Loan Eligibility calculators.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website. We are committed to protecting your privacy. In short — we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information you enter into our calculators.

Simple version: All calculations happen entirely in your browser. Your financial data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers.
02

Information We Collect

We collect minimal, non-personal information automatically when you visit our website:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website URL
  • General geographic location (country/city level only, via IP address)
  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)

This information is collected by third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) and is used solely to understand how users interact with our site so we can improve it.

03

No Personal Data Collection

FinCalc India does not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Financial figures you enter into our calculators (salary, loan amount, investment amount, etc.)
  • Bank account or payment information
  • Aadhaar number, PAN, or any government ID
  • Location beyond city/country level

All calculator inputs and results are processed locally in your web browser using JavaScript. No data is transmitted to our servers.

We do not require you to create an account or log in to use any feature on this website.

04

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device. We use the following types:

  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics uses cookies to track page views, session duration, and user behaviour in aggregate form. This helps us understand which calculators are most used.
  • Advertising cookies — Google AdSense places cookies to show relevant advertisements based on your browsing history and interests.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect the display of advertisements but will not affect the functionality of our calculators.

How to manage cookies: In Chrome — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies. In Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.

Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
